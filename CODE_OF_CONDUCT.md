# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be useful, respectful, and welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of different experience levels.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- Assume good intent when possible.
- Help make AI tooling safer and more practical.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment or personal attacks
- Publishing private data
- Encouraging abusive scraping
- Spam or irrelevant promotion
- Discriminatory language

## Enforcement

Maintainers may close issues, remove comments, or block users who repeatedly act against the health of the project.
